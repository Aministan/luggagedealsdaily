What to expect on Black Friday

Luggage prices often move from Black Friday through Cyber Monday, sometimes more than once. Open the merchant page that day and use the price printed there. A compare-at on a product page is the merchant's own reference price, not a holiday rate this site promises. Check the size, the model name, and the return window before you buy. This page does not estimate a weekend percent off.
